Currently Working On

BugFishing:
Open Waters

BugFishing: Open Waters will be a relaxing fishing game, where the fish are bugs! The player will follow a frog named Jerm as he takes on his grandfrog's old bugfishing gear, including a beat up airship. Catch bugs, discover various biomes and species, and upgrade your gear, all while exploring the area and meeting new friends.

This game is meant to be an expansion on my previous project, BugFishing. I plan to remake the game using the techniques I have since learned, as well as adding a great deal of content.

Because of the larger scope, this game's development will include much more planning and documentation, which will be published here as progress is made.
